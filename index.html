Franz Ferdinand

In 1914 after the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria-Hungary, a web of alliances, as well as deep seated rivalies between European countries caused the incident to become a full-blown world war.

Allied and Central Powers

There were two sides: Germany, Austria-Hungary, the Ottoman Empire, and Bulgaria made up the Central Powers, and France, Briatain, Russia, and the United States were the main Allied Powers.

Industrialization

World War I took place shortly after the Industrial Revolution. This introduced new, never-seen-before weapons, such as tanks, machine guns, and other devices that caused unprecedented amounts of damage during the war.

Lost generation

These were young men who were psychologically or physically damaged by the war, and could not return to normal civilian life. They also felt a disconnect from the civilians, who had no idea of the terrors of the front lines; who saw war as a courageous adventure.
